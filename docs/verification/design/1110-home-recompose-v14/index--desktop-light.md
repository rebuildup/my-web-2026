This visual review covers the full-page layout, from the hero section to the footer.

### DEFECTS:
1.  **Imbalanced Visual Weight (Section 00):** The high-saturation color blocks in the "Site Guide" are the most dominant elements on the entire page. They create a "visual wall" that makes the actual content below (Section 01/02) feel like secondary fine print.
2.  **Weak Information Hierarchy in Lists (Section 01):** Under "Where I am now," the project titles (e.g., *FlashBeat*, *pc-setup*) lack sufficient typographic distinction from the metadata below them. The "GitHub →" links are too small and repetitive, creating "link fatigue."
3.  **Fragmented Mid-Section Grid:** The "Rebuildup" card and the "Mic Level Checker" banner break the established editorial grid. They feel like external advertisements rather than integrated content, creating a jarring shift from the minimalist aesthetic.
4.  **Alignment Break (Section 03):** The "Contact me" button in the black band is right-aligned, while 95% of the page content is strictly left-aligned. This forces an unnecessary horizontal eye-swing at the very end of the scroll.
5.  **Micro-Typography Legibility:** The text "planned Content — コンテンツ: 長めのノートや記事..." under the color grid is excessively small and lacks sufficient contrast/container, making it look like a technical glitch rather than intentional information.

### OPPORTUNITIES:
1.  **Data Visualization (Ambient Signals):** The "GitHub Activity" section is currently a raw text dump. Replacing the commit counts with a simple sparkline or horizontal bar would better suit the "minimalist editorial" style and increase scannability.
2.  **Color Reinforcement:** The colors introduced in the "Site Guide" (Yellow for Writing, Pink for Design) could be used as subtle 2px accent borders or category labels in the "Ambient Signals" section to create a cohesive design system.
3.  **Rhythm and Breathing Room:** Section 02 (Site Status) is very dense. Increasing the vertical gutter between "Platform health" and "Reactions" would prevent the two-column layout from feeling cramped.
4.  **Footer Scale:** The "04" in the footer is unnecessarily large. Reducing its scale would maintain the focus on the "Contact" CTA immediately above it.

### OVERALL VERDICT:
A sophisticated bilingual layout that successfully blends developer utility with editorial minimalism, though it suffers from "data-dump" density in the middle sections and an overly aggressive color grid at the top.
