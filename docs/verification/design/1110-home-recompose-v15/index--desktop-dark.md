This is a rigorous visual review of the full-page layout.

### **DEFECTS:**
1.  **Grid Fragmentation (Section 00):** The "Site Guide" grid lacks vertical alignment between rows. Row 1 has 3 columns, Row 2 has 3 columns, but Row 3 switches to 2 wider columns. This creates "stair-stepping" vertical seams that break the editorial rigor established in the hero.
2.  **Excessive Vertical Stacking (Section 01):** The "Where I am now" section repeats the same layout (Title, Description, Stats, Link) four times vertically. This creates high scroll fatigue and "monotony" (Point 5). These projects should likely be a multi-column grid or a tighter list to respect the user's scroll.
3.  **Jarring Background Shift (Sponsor/Ad Section):** The transition from the black background of Section 01 to a full-width white section for the QR Generator is too high-contrast. It feels like a third-party advertisement injection rather than a cohesive part of a personal site. It breaks the "color-field meaning" (Point 3).
4.  **Information Density Imbalance (Section 02):** The "Site Status" and "GitHub Activity" sections are extremely dense compared to the "Now" section. The tiny "commit" text and "PR" links create a visual "gray block" of text that is difficult to scan compared to the airy headers above.
5.  **Alignment Broken in Footer:** In the "04 - EDITION" footer, the baseline of the large "04" does not align with the baseline of the "my-web-2026" text or the "Source/Portfolio" links, making the bottom of the page feel "bottom-heavy" and unfinished.

### **OPPORTUNITIES:**
1.  **Icon Redundancy:** The yellow ghost icon appears in the Hero and again as a small floating element near the Site Guide. Consider removing the second instance to reduce visual noise.
2.  **Call-to-Action Color:** The blue buttons ("QRを作る" and "教材を生成") introduce a new primary color that doesn't appear anywhere else in the brand palette (Yellow, Pink, Teal). Bringing these into the established palette would improve cohesion.
3.  **Proximity in Status Section:** The "Platform Health" dots (External boundary, Internal data, etc.) are far from their labels. Using a subtle leader line or bringing the "reachable" badges closer to the text would improve readability.
4.  **Bilingual Hierarchy:** While the EN/JP pairing is clean, the English sub-headers (e.g., "Where I am now") are significantly larger than the Japanese headers in some sections but smaller in others (Section 03). Standardizing this ratio would polish the "editorial" feel.

### **OVERALL VERDICT:**
A sophisticated editorial concept that suffers from "scroll fatigue" in the middle and a jarring mid-page color shift that breaks the established minimalist dark-mode identity.
